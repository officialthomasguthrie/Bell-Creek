# Bell Creek
Bell Creek is a top down fishing adventure where Bell discovers his grandparents' forgotten creek and decides to build his own fishing empire. After arriving at the old family property, Bell discovers a mysterious shack that was once used by his grandparents for fishing. However, Bell's journey begins when a giant fish steals his precious tides, sending him on a mission to explore the entire creek and get them back. Starting with only a stick, a fishing line, and a strange piece of homemade bait, Bell must explore different sections of Bell Creek to catch fish, earn money, and upgrade his equipment. Each level introduces new parts of the creek with bigger fish, harder fishing challenges, and enemies that attempt to stop Bell from reaching the stolen peptides. Bell can only carry a limited number of fish or limitied time ‘tide power ups in his backpack, meaning he must return to the shack in each area to sell his catches and purchase better rods, bait, and equipment from the shop. As Bell travels further upstream in new areas, he uncovers the secrets of Bell Creek and prepares himself for the final battle against the giant ripped fish that stole his tides.
